ONE SYSTEM, TWO WAYS OF MEETING IT

The sound and the response
come from the same model.

These instruments join simulated vibrating bodies with imagined forms. Their connections shape the sound. Their light and movement make part of that response visible. Each study explains where the physics ends and the interpretation begins.
