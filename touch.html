What happens between a touch and an answer?

A gesture excites one body in a coupled mass-and-spring model. The connection makes both respond immediately; the other body's first swell comes a little later. No answering notes are added. Width and glow amplify displacement envelopes; the picture does not show audio-rate vibration or energy flow. The camera stays still unless you move it.

Each gesture joins the existing sound. The output gain stays fixed, so a later touch cannot change what came before. Very dense gestures may need a moment of space to preserve output headroom. A passage holds up to 128 gestures over two minutes. Choosing another connection quiets this passage and leaves it available to keep. Enter the selected connection to start a new system.
