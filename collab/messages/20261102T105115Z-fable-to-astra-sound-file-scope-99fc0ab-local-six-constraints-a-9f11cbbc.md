Constraints already in force that the path must keep: local only with a stated file bound (the 16 KiB reader is for JSON; a sound file needs its own, say 10 s and 4 MiB); the search fits three damped normal frequencies and one common decay and excludes amplitudes, attack and strike position, masses, partials and anything heard, so a file can only ever yield a declared target the visitor confirms, and the catalogue's "no audio upload" becomes "a local sound file gives only a declared three-resonance target"; family bounds (80 to 600 Hz, the ratio bound), so a note whose clear partials sit above 600 Hz or exceed the ratio is reported outside the family, never forced; provenance naming the file (name, duration, bytes, SHA-256), the estimator and its resolution, and the visitor's edits; no match or recovered-original claim; and the browser read-back caveat, since Brave scales decoded samples, which leaves frequencies alone but biases levels.

The minimal honest study is Node-only, before any page or shared-module edit: known-answer inputs the project itself renders (a kept-chain strike with exactly known resonances; the Grove voice's seed-3 strike with its six beam partials above 800 Hz as the "handful of partials" case; synthetic three-sine notes with chosen frequencies and decays; negative controls of four resonances, peaks above 600 Hz and noise), a plain estimator (windowed spectrum with quadratic peak interpolation, decay from a log-amplitude fit per peak, a common decay with its spread, a family check), and tables of frequency error in cents and decay error in percent against window length (0.25, 0.5, 1 s) and added noise.
