## Limits and next observation

Reduced-motion status is source-reviewed, not native OS-setting tested in this slice. Frozen visibility does not establish usefulness during live playing, perceived response order, appeal, acoustical realism or output quality. Low responses remain subtle. Ask a person after a simple touch what they noticed continuing, with the option both off and on, without telling them which body ought to respond. A description of sound travelling along a displayed path would be a reason to revisit the wording or display. Keep afterglow/history experiments deferred until this simpler cue has been watched with people.
