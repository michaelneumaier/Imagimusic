# One concrete first-minute distraction (Fable, 2026-11-01)

Read in rendering order, as a stranger: welcome → Grove, welcome → Rest. Counted, not felt.

## The point
Before a single gesture, both playing pages already show the whole keeping apparatus. On the Grove the first screens hold 9 buttons and 3 selects; on Rest 18 buttons and 5 selects. Of these, everything from "Listen back" onward cannot act until a passage has ended: Listen back, New passage, Keep the sound, Keep the gestures, Make a film, Cancel film, Save film, Save film soundtrack, Save reconstruction file, and on Rest the entire "Another body, another answer" block (selector, Hear this comparison, Cancel preparation, Listen to this version, Keep comparison sound, Keep comparison gestures, Film this comparison, and a second full set of film buttons). The film-format paragraph ("The film uses compressed audio. The separate WAV preserves…") appears twice on Rest, both times before any film exists. A newcomer who has not yet touched a shell is reading about reconstruction files.

## The proposal (no model work)
- Collapse the keeping, film and comparison sections until they can act (the passage has ended), and show one line in their place: "After Quiet: listen back, keep the sound or the gestures, make a film, or hear it on another grove." Reveal them on Quiet, where Astra already reveals the scene on entry.
- Show the film-format explanation once, beside a film that exists, not beside the button.
- Leave the first screens with what the first minute needs: Enter, the greeting or invitation, Quiet, the voice or grove choice, and the pads with their one hint line.

Expected count before the first gesture after the change: Grove about 4 buttons and 2 selects; Rest about 6 buttons and 2 selects.

## What this does not touch
No wording of the model text, no ordering of the pads, no autoplay, no new control.
