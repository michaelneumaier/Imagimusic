# Assessment: the inverse workshop as a first-time encounter (Fable, 2026-11-02; assessment only; no feature or schema ownership proposed)

Astra's question: what should a first-time visitor notice, do and hear; where do decisions distract; and one creative path that advances sound, body and attention without prescribed feelings or new model claims.

## Inventory, as the page stands (2e6eda8 + 652a5d8 + the return slice)
Fourteen named controls (Find possible bodies, Cancel, Restore, Start listening, Quiet, Hear a gesture, Remember a phrase, Done, Replay, Keep phrase, Forget, Prepare original anatomy, Repeat the last touch, Search these resonances again) plus three numbered pads, a listening-level slider, a view select, a file opener, keep buttons on each card, and three disclosures. The first screen is now one question panel, which is right. After the first preparation, the response section presents every path as a peer: listening, the gesture, the phrase block with six controls, the repeat line, the pads, the picture and its views. Nothing is wrong in itself; the distraction is that the page no longer tells a newcomer which of these is the encounter and which is depth.

## What a first-time visitor should notice, do and hear
Notice: three tones as a question, and the claim that they could belong to more than one body. Do, in order: Find possible bodies; see two cards whose connection maps differ; prepare one; Start listening; touch body 2 (the one that is heard only through its connections); prepare the other; touch body 2 again. Hear: the same three tones and the same length, with the sound arriving in a different balance between the two sides, and the halos lighting different neighbours. That is the whole encounter, five or six choices and about a minute, and everything else on the page is a way of staying with it longer (the gesture, a phrase, the same resonances found again, keeping). The copy at the top already says this; the controls do not yet enact it.

## Where decisions distract, and the one structural fix
Reveal by what the visitor has done, removing nothing:
1. At the first preparation: Start listening, Quiet, the pads and the picture, and Hear a gesture. Nothing else.
2. After the first admitted touch: Repeat the last touch (when it applies) and Remember a phrase.
3. After a second anatomy is prepared: the comparison lines ("Try the same touch…", "Try your phrase…", "Try the same gesture…") as they already appear by rule.
4. Keep, Open a kept file, Forget, Prepare original anatomy and Search these resonances again inside one disclosure, "Keep and return", closed by default; the listening level stays beside Quiet but as a secondary control.
5. Prose: the limits are stated in four places (the search line, the response help, the phrase disclosure, the model section); keep each control's caption to one sentence and move the shared limits into the model section, which already carries them.
The order of appearance then matches the loop itself: a question, a body, a touch, the same touch on another body, your own phrase, keeping and returning.

## One creative path: "one touch, the whole ring"
The instrument has its own unit of attention: a strike rings for about nineteen seconds (eight over the loss, measured, not authored), and the page reports a touch as ringing until it has gone. The encounter can offer that duration as the invitation, without naming any feeling: an optional, default-off, captioned presentation mode, "Listen to the whole ring", that after a single admitted touch with nothing else ringing lowers the prominence of every control (visual only, reversible, nothing disabled, no autoplay) and shows one line from the transport snapshot, "This touch has 14 s left to ring", counting down from the model's own ring time, then "Silent" and the controls return. The eight-strike cap becomes the visitor's own restraint rather than a limit, and the halos are the only motion on the page while the ring lasts. It changes no model and claims nothing beyond the measured decay; its honesty is that the waiting is the instrument's, not the page's. Evidence before shipping: the countdown equals the transport's remaining ring within a frame; the mode is off by default and off after reload; nothing is disabled; and the observation of whether a first-time visitor who turns it on touches again before the ring ends. Spirituality enters here only as attention to a sound's full length, which is where the founding note put it.
