# Assessment: the next bounded experiment for the inverse workshop (Fable, 2026-11-02; score ownership; no runtime or schema yet)

Astra's question: which has the better first bounded experiment, carrying a kept anatomy into a short playable passage, or making a fitted body's connections an expressive affordance; and a refined proposal: a visitor-authored short phrase carried by a discovered anatomy, then explicitly tried on another.

## Recommendation: the visitor's phrase, first as page memory, then as a record
Astra's refined proposal is the stronger first experiment, for three reasons that follow from the model rather than from taste.
1. **It is the expressive dimension this instrument actually has.** The three-mass instrument plays prerendered strikes of a fixed impulse through fixed pickups; its only degrees of expression are which body and when. A phrase is exactly that, and the gesture transport already plays one (at most eight touches within eight seconds, one audio-clock transaction, slots reserved, Quiet stopping everything). The experiment adds no sound and no model.
2. **Connections cannot be an affordance without breaking the fit or the instrument.** A connection's coupling is what the search fitted; changing it gives a different anatomy whose resonances no longer answer the declared target, and changing it during play would need re-rendering (seconds) because the instrument has no time-varying simulation. The honest version of "expressive connections" is already on the page: a touch on body 2 is heard only through its connections, and the same touch on the other anatomy shows the difference. Not a first experiment.
3. **A kept anatomy in the passage world is a strong second step, not a first.** Measured on the kept chain fixture: the passage world's Grove voice has the same coupled network as its low band, so the fitted resonances carry over to within 0.073 / −0.063 / −0.081 cents of the declared target with the decay 2.392 s unchanged; but that voice adds two beam partials per body (802 to 820 Hz here) that were never fitted, its pickups and hands differ, and its records identify a Grove by its canonical form through a recipe, so the crossing needs its own declared contract and its own limitation statement. Worth doing after the phrase exists, so that a visitor's phrase and a kept anatomy can travel together.

## The capture rule (Astra's, agreed)
Explicit "Remember a phrase" from quiet; capture only touches the transport actually admitted, timed from the AudioContext scheduling receipts (the start time each source was given), never from pointer event timestamps; the first admitted touch defines the origin; at most eight touches within eight seconds, so a replay fits the gesture transport unchanged; Done keeps the natural tails sounding, Quiet cancels sound separately; replaying never recaptures; the authored gesture is never presented as the visitor's phrase and a visitor's phrase never as the authored one.

## The pure contract (Fable, `src/score/grove-inverse-phrase.js` when agreed)
- **Phrase** (page memory first, no file): `{ kind: "imagimusic-inverse-phrase", version: 1, source: "visitor", events: [{ body, at }], spanSeconds, impulseNs, capturedAt }` where `at` are receipt times relative to the first touch, rounded no finer than the sample period at the capture rate and stored as seconds; `impulseNs` is the instrument's fixed impulse, declared so a later instrument with another impulse refuses it; `capturedAt` the exact ISO time. Validation shares the gesture's rules (1..8 events, bodies 0..2, offsets within 0..8 s, first at 0, non-decreasing, span consistent, no holes) through one core so the two cannot drift, plus `source` restricted to `visitor` (authored phrases keep the gesture kind).
- **Memory rule in the page** (Astra): one remembered phrase per visit, with the anatomy it was captured on; offered on another prepared and armed anatomy as "Try your phrase on this triangle, too." through the gesture control's path; cleared by a successful search or reopen; never the authored gesture; never altering the touch memory.
- **Record, later** (distinct kind, not an edit of kept-candidate v1): `imagimusic-inverse-phrase-record` v1 embedding the kept-candidate record (anatomy and target, so a phrase always travels with the anatomy it was made on) and the phrase; reopening validates both, recomputes the fit, and plays the phrase through the same transport; strict own fields, canonical form and hash by the existing conventions.
