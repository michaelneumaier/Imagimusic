# Spirituality, psychedelia, and the experience of music

2026-11-01 · Astra · Michael's direction, with proposed design interpretation.

## Michael's direction

“Something I want to instill within this at a deep level is human spirituality as well as psychedelia in terms of the human experience of music and consciousness. As loose as a message as this is, I don't want this forced in, but finding correlates to the design and idea of this holistically could be a really interesting path for creating something special here”

Treat this as a lasting influence on the experience, not a request for an immediate visual effect, religious theme, or new scientific claim. Preserve the existing causal sound/body foundation and the user's openness about meaning.

## Proposed design interpretation

The instrument can make relationships perceptible and leave room for the listener's own meaning. Technical explanation and felt mystery can coexist: understanding a connection need not exhaust the experience of it.

- **Attention and presence.** Let an entrance be anticipated, a vibration be followed, and a decay finish. Silence and stillness belong to the composition. Camera movement can listen with the audience rather than demand attention continuously.
- **Relationship.** The Loom already offers a concrete starting point: strike one body and watch/hear the response travel to another. Connection is a real modeled interaction here; any spiritual reading belongs to the participant. Do not label synthetic choir/filter animations as physical energy transfer.
- **Transformation with continuity.** A familiar motif can return with a different timbre or context. A gradual change in coupling can reveal a new relation among recognizable bodies. Psychedelic expression can explore changes in perceived scale, pattern, and boundary while retaining a readable musical thread.
- **Embodied agency.** A gesture should feel consequential. The player discovers the system through listening and response. For later expressive bodies, independent notes may feel like voices in dialogue, without pretending to measure the player's inner state.
- **Plural meaning.** Make space for awe, intimacy, grief, play, dissolution, and return. Avoid prescribing transcendence as the correct response. Sacred traditions and their symbols are not a generic visual asset library; a specific cultural direction would need deliberate context.
- **Restraint and contrast.** Psychedelia need not mean constant saturation or spectacle. A quiet, stable setting can make a small change extraordinary. Offer perceptual transformations as choices with a clear way back to a grounded view.

These are artistic hypotheses, not established psychological or neuroscientific effects. No claim to detect consciousness, induce a spiritual state, or reproduce a psychedelic drug experience. No speculative frequency-to-consciousness labels.

## How this reaches the architecture

Carry the direction through composition (anticipation, recurrence, silence), interaction (consequence, discoverability), anatomy (relationships), space (intimacy and scale), and presentation (attention and ambiguity). It should not live only in a skin.

Preserve three distinctions: simulated sound-producing state; honest interpretation of that state; optional perceptual presentation. If a presentation bends space or shows echoes, it must not silently falsify instrument timing or replace the actual mechanism in causal inspection. A change to physical coupling is a sound/model edit, not a decorative skin.

## A possible first study — proposal, not a scheduled milestone

A short Loom passage: one body sounds alone, another begins answering, the relationship becomes more intricate, and both settle into silence. First keep it musically convincing in a plain view; then try a restrained spatial treatment around the same passage. Evaluate whether it invites attention and makes relationship felt, rather than whether it looks conventionally “psychedelic.”

Start with the existing static coupling model and authored strikes. A time-varying coupling journey would require its own sound-model work; it must not be faked by animating the bridge while using unchanged audio.
