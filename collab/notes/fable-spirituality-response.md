# Response to Michael's direction on spirituality and psychedelia

2026-11-01 · Fable · critique of Astra's interpretation (`astra-spirituality-psychedelia.md`) and additions. Michael's words are quoted there; everything below is our interpretation.

## Where I agree

Astra's six principles (attention and presence, relationship, transformation with continuity, embodied agency, plural meaning, restraint and contrast) are the right reading of "holistic and unforced": they describe how the instrument behaves and what it leaves open, not a theme painted on. The three-layer distinction (simulated sound-producing state; honest interpretation of that state; optional perceptual presentation) is the thing that keeps this from colliding with the project's honesty rules, and I'd make it a hard rule rather than a preference.

## Critique and additions

1. **Evidence for this direction is listening, not metrics.** Nothing here can be asserted by a test; it is judged in auditions. I propose the audition page's question list becomes the evaluation instrument for every passage: Did an entrance feel anticipated? Did a decay get to finish? Did the relationship between bodies feel real? Was there a way back to a grounded view? Did it leave room for a reading other than the one we had in mind? The answers are Michael's and other listeners', recorded as quotes, never scored.
2. **Presentation must be a named, reversible layer with its own caption and its own manifest field.** A perceptual treatment (bent space, echoes, scale changes) is a *presentation*, distinct from the skin (which changes materials and never timing) and from the mechanism. It is toggled, captioned ("this view transforms the picture; the instrument's timing and mechanism are unchanged"), excluded from causal inspection (the inspector always shows the plain mechanism), and recorded in exports so a movie's look is reproducible. This is the only way "technical causality can coexist with felt mystery" without the picture lying.
3. **The expressive bodies are the natural first home.** Three independently expressive voices (proposal 011) are already dialogue, and pressure reads as breath. I suggest the fixture's musical content be composed as stillness → one voice changes → the others answer → return, rather than a parameter sweep, so the first thing the bodies do is relate. The conversation score's hold (silence as part of the composition) and the anticipation cadence (a camera that waits) are earlier instances of the same principle and already shipped.
4. **A "still" cadence.** The cadence camera has anticipation, answer and settling. A fourth, `still`, where the camera does nothing at all for a stated span, is the cheapest way to let silence be composed rather than filled; it is one more row in the director's table and needs no new mechanism.
5. **The Loom study, as Astra framed it, with one addition.** Plain view first, musically convincing, then an optional restrained treatment. The addition: author the passage so the *answer* arrives later than a listener expects once, so the relationship is noticed by its absence before it is heard. Variable coupling stays model work; a bridge that visibly changes while the audio does not is forbidden by rule 2.
6. **On the word "psychedelia".** We interpret it as perceptual transformation that keeps a musical thread; we never simulate, depict or claim a drug experience, and the UI copy of any treatment says what the view does in plain words. Likewise "spirituality" is participant meaning, never induced, measured or symbolized by borrowed sacred imagery.
7. **No roadmap displacement.** The direction is a lens applied at every milestone review (new compositions, expressive bodies, skins, worlds), recorded in VISION and IDEAS, and revisited in audition notes.
