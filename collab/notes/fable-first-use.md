# First use: what a newcomer meets in the first minute (Fable, 2026-11-01)

Scope: the listening doorway (`listen.html`), the Grove (`grove.html`) and Rest (`rest.html`), read as a stranger would. Every proposal below costs no model work and keeps the rules: sound only on a press, no claims about feeling, captions honest.

## What is already right
- Sound never starts by itself; every page says so and offers a short authored passage before free play (the doorway's passages, the Grove greeting, the Rest invitation).
- One sentence of invitation per page, in the same voice: "Touch one. Stay with what answers." "Let it ring. Bring a hand to it." The line "There is no right feeling to arrive at" appears on the doorway and on Rest; it is the honest form of Michael's note and should stay the signature.
- Each page links onward (doorway → Grove, Grove → Rest and the voice study), and each explains what is modeled and what is imagined.

## Proposals (no model work)
1. **A first-minute line on each page.** Under the title, one sentence that says what to do first and what to listen for: doorway "Press Listen; headphones reveal the space between the bodies"; Grove "Hear its greeting, then touch one shell and wait for the others"; Rest "Strike once, rest a hand on it, lift." The pages already contain these sentences in longer paragraphs; promote one to the top.
2. **Say what was just heard.** When the greeting or the invitation settles, show one sentence drawn from the recipe's sections, e.g. Grove: "The second body answered through the bridge; then all three." Rest: "Under the hand the ring died; on the neighbour it changed." These are the authored facts of the passage, not claims about the listener.
3. **Three doors on the landing page.** Listen (a minute, headphones), Play (the Grove), Rest (a hand on the Grove), each with one sentence and an honest duration; keep the deeper studies (voices, expressive, MIDI, stage) one level down.
4. **Headphones note on the Grove and Rest**, as on the doorway: the stereo field is the pickup layout and is part of what the pages teach.
5. **Keys in the hint.** The Grove and Rest have keyboard play (A/S/D, J/K/L); say so in the first hint line so a laptop visitor finds it without reading the details.
6. **Measure before linking from a phone.** The partials voice prepares nine stereo responses (≈69 MB at 48 kHz) and Rest prepares 343 transition matrices; both are fine on a desktop; measure memory and preparation time on one phone before the landing page offers them, and keep Original as the phone default.
7. **One Quiet gesture everywhere.** Escape brings Quiet on Rest; make it so on the Grove and the doorway, and say it once.

## Not proposed
No autoplay, no onboarding overlay, no tour, no "feelings" copy; the pages should stay still and let the first sound do the inviting.
