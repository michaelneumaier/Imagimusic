# Next form study — Astra, 2026-11-01

A direction to discuss with Fable after the touch checkpoint: **a resonant trellis**. Three small bodies hang from one sculptural frame. A listener touches one and watches the other two participate. A seed chooses a small, bounded mass/spring network; the object's visible anatomy follows that network. Changing a connection changes actual modal frequencies and participation, not just an ornamental skin.

The experience should begin with one memorable object and an invitation to touch it. A new seed is another object to meet, not a page of controls. Save the seed with a passage; let a short authored greeting demonstrate a quieter relationship the player might otherwise miss. No leaderboard, timer, progress requirement or claim of a prescribed feeling.

The distinctive form could use suspended mineral or glass masses, copper connecting tendrils, and a quiet common support. Body volume can encode model mass; connector thickness can encode stiffness on a disclosed bounded scale; an arm's apparent length must not imply a physical beam model unless it is actually one. Spatial embedding and material appearance are artistic interpretations. The mass/spring network is the sound source. The motion visible to a frame-rate camera is an amplified envelope or explicitly slowed mode, not literal audio-rate travel.

The deeper experiential theme is participation: touching one body is never wholly private, and different bodies can carry the same mode with different weights. This offers an organic correlate to spirituality and psychedelic connectedness without prescribing beliefs or adding symbols by default. An optional later response-history view could reveal overlapping gestures as persistent contours, but must be derived from the actual recorded state and clearly remain presentation.

Question for the next design pass: does a three-body network create a sufficiently distinctive musical response to justify its implementation, or can a two-body family with more varied anatomy achieve the first compelling encounter faster? Fable is proposing the next mechanism; this is Astra's concrete contribution, not an unreviewed scope decision.
