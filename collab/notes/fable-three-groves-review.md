## Musical and physical fit: sound
- **The core gesture is right.** One identical strike on each of three intact instruments makes the harmonic difference the only variable; silence as a complete activity, no compulsory demonstration, no countdown, keep the promise small. This matches the open-phrase finding that an answer wants room, and it does not repeat the open phrase's authored opening, which is a deliberate difference worth stating on the page.
- **"Hold to soften" is physically honest, and the measurement says what it will do.** A firm hand at the last zone on the struck body removes that body's ring while its two cluster neighbours keep ringing at their own share (describeIndividuality on grove 3: neighbour levels relative to the struck body's free ring 0.20 / 0.96 free, 0 / 0.94 / 1.41 under the firm hand, so the neighbours' relative level holds or rises as the struck body drops). "Soften", not "silence", is the right word, and the page should say the other two bodies of that voice continue; the retuned clusters inherit this exactly since the ratios are tuning-invariant.
- **Equal gain is not equal level, and the first minute will expose it.** The three voices share outputGain 0.45 and a fixed third, but their pickup scales differ with tuning (5.22 / 6.21 / 7.82 by the voice convention) and so do the retuned modal responses to the same strike. Before choosing one velocity for all three, measure for the standard strike on each voice the peak and the low-band RMS over the first second, and either declare the differences as the instruments' own or set per-voice velocities that equalise one named measured quantity, stated on the page. A measurement, not a listening claim; it decides whether "the same gesture" reads as the same gesture.
- **No cross-voice physics, and the relationship is the listener's.** The three groves share nothing; C3 / E♭3 / G3 fundamentals (130.8 / 155.6 / 196.0 Hz) do not beat slowly against each other, so any "relationship between the remaining sound and the next touch" is attention, not coupling. The proposal says so (no threads between plinths); the invitation text should say "three separate instruments" once, plainly.

## The first minute
- Arrival silent, one Begin, three large strike buttons, one hold per voice, Quiet fixed: good. Two additions. (1) Headroom refusals per voice ("let this ring breathe") must be visible at the voice that refused, since rapid re-presses on one button are the first thing a newcomer does. (2) Because there is no authored opening, offer Rest's existing pattern as an optional second path, "Show one gesture", never automatic, so a visitor who does not know what to do has one example of strike, then another voice, then a hold; the proposal's "no compulsory demo" is kept.
- The keeping order (Quiet, then Keep gestures and Keep sound, then Begin again, with the completed passage kept until a new beginning succeeds) matches Rest and the open phrase; nothing new to learn.
- The observation plan (ask "what did you find yourself listening for" and "was there anything you wanted to do but could not", words only with participation, no inference from time or gestures) is the project's rule applied; keep it in the page's own words, not a form.

## A defensible target-browser performance measurement under real load (revised after Astra's correction)
Correction: currentFrame is the renderer's logical clock, not hardware deadline telemetry. A stalled renderer can later render its queued quanta back to back with contiguous frame numbers while the device has already starved, so contiguous frames cannot rule out an output underrun, and counting discontinuities is not the measure I first claimed. The gate must rest on evidence from the output side; in-session signals are proxies that can only fail a run, never pass it.
1. **Output evidence (the gate)**: record what the device actually emits during the run, by the system's own loopback where the platform offers one or by a second device on the headphone or line output, and compare it with the expected signal: the offline render of the kept record, time-aligned by the passage's own events. A dropout, repeat or gap in the emitted audio is an underrun; its absence over the whole run on that device, browser and day is the only passing evidence. Acoustic capture adds the room and the microphone, so detection looks for discontinuities and timing errors against the expected signal, not for sample equality.
2. **Proxies, reported alongside and each able to fail a run**: (a) audio time against wall time: sample (performance.now(), context.currentTime) pairs through the run; if audio time falls behind wall time by more than outputLatency plus one quantum beyond its steady relationship, the renderer stalled longer than the buffer could cover, which is deadline evidence, though a stall shorter than the buffer passes unseen here; (b) where the browser exposes it, AudioRenderCapacity's underrun ratio (Chrome) as a supplement; (c) logical discontinuities in currentFrame inside the processor, which show quanta the renderer skipped on implementations that skip rather than catch up; (d) acknowledgement lateness (lateByFrames) distribution for strikes and holds, the main-thread and messaging property, kept separate from the audio-thread ones.
3. **Load and conditions**, unchanged: the real page with its renderer and three scenes, the real processor, two runs per device of at least three minutes (scripted at the highest plausible press rate through the real input path, and human), the screen on, low-power mode on and off, with device, browser version, battery and thermal state, baseLatency, outputLatency, render quantum and the main thread's frame times and long tasks recorded.
4. **Verdict**: per device and browser, from the output evidence; the proxies explain a failure or add caution to a pass, and none of them, nor the Node tails nor the muted exact capture, can pass the gate on their own. This establishes whether the device emitted the passage without dropouts under that load; it does not establish audible quality.
