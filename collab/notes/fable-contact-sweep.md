# Contact strength and place under the powered driver (Fable, 2026-11-01; measurements only, no listening claim)

Same source in every row (the study's declared sinusoid at the body's tuning, 2 s on, 3 s released, amplitude fixed per grove), no normalization, 8 kHz. "rel/forced" is the RMS of the released interval over the RMS of the forced interval: a shape measure of build-and-release, not loudness. Contacts remain through the release.

Each doubling of c costs about 5.7 dB on every grove, and the build-and-release shape is nearly constant from c ≈ 0.5 upward. At the tip, where the bridges attach and the driver's partials all move, strength only scales the response down.

## 2. Place on the driven body shapes more than strength does (c = 1 and 4 N·s/m)
| place | c = 1: peak vs free, rel/forced | c = 4: peak vs free, rel/forced |
|---|---|---|
| seed 3 upper u = 0.3 | −1.0 dB, 0.48 | −3.7 dB, 0.37 |
| seed 3 middle u = 0.6 (where the driver acts) | −7.7 dB, 0.27 | −16.5 dB, 0.20 |
| seed 3 tip u = 1.0 | −17.7 dB, 0.19 | −29.1 dB, 0.19 |
| seed 7 upper / middle / tip at c = 1 | −1.0, 0.53 / −8.0, 0.45 / −18.4, 0.43 | at c = 4: −3.6, 0.47 / −17.2, 0.43 / −29.6, 0.41 |
| seed 11 upper / middle / tip at c = 1 | −0.7, 0.44 / −6.3, 0.28 / −15.7, 0.17 | at c = 4: −2.6, 0.36 / −14.7, 0.18 / −26.0, 0.17 |

A hand at the upper zone leaves the response within 1–4 dB of free while changing the release shape (seed 3: 0.56 → 0.48 → 0.37 as c grows). This is the "intermediate region": not a strength, a place.

## 3. A hand on the neighbour preserves the response and changes what remains
Across c from 0.25 to 60: seed 3 peak within −0.3…+2.0 dB of free, rel/forced 0.52 → 0.70; seed 7 within ±1 dB, 0.57 → 0.54; seed 11 −4…−8 dB, 0.32 → 0.59. On seeds 3 and 11 the released interval grows relative to the forced one as the neighbour's hand firms: the hand on the other body changes the relationship after release without taking the driven response away.

## Proposal: at most three strengths, and one place
- Keep **firm = 60 N·s/m** as the reference stop (the existing case) and **light = 12** as it is; add **medium = 1 N·s/m** only if a strength step is wanted on the Rest page later (−18 dB at the tip; at the upper zone −1 dB with a visible release change). Units: viscous coefficient per unit mode-shape value, as the recipe states.
- For the next audition, vary **place** rather than strength: four cases at one source, free / upper hand c = 1 / neighbour hand c = 4 / tip hand c = 60 (reference), on seeds 3 and 11 where the measured contrasts are largest.
