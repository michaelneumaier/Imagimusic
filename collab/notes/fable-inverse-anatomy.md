# Proposal: a spatial anatomy for the inverse workshop (Fable, 2026-11-02; proposal only; Astra owns the encounter)

Astra's question: give "A sound, several bodies" visibly connected, touchable bodies whose motion stays tied to the three-mass response, with form and layout explicitly artistic; no extra partials, no false acoustic geometry; reuse the Grove's visual vocabulary if it helps while keeping this instrument's identity; make a changed connection discoverable through touch rather than through a parameter panel.

## What the three-mass instrument can honestly show
The transport's snapshot gives, for each active strike, its body, its age and the instrument's `frame(body, age)`: three displacements and three declared low-band envelopes with the ending fade. The grove snapshot gives the layout (chain in a line, triangle on a circle), the three bodies and the bridges with their couplings. Nothing else exists in this model: no strike position along a body, no hand, no partials. A picture may therefore show: where the bodies are and how they are joined (layout and bridges), how strongly each is joined (coupling), which body was touched and when (strike marks), and how each body's low-band envelope rises and decays after a touch (the envelope), including the envelope of a body that was not touched. That last one is the whole point of the workshop made visible: the same touch on two anatomies with the same resonances sends the ring to different neighbours.

## Reuse the Grove vocabulary, with three deliberate differences
`createGroveScene(grove, { bodyLabels })` and `applyGroveScene(scene, { envelope, strikes }, holds, positions, options)` already draw bodies with a shell, a core, a floor halo and a label, bridges as sagging brass tubes between attachment points, a strike ring with the remembered-dot option, and an envelope-driven pulse (capped at 20 % widening), emissive and halo (with the Follow-the-ring gain). The inverse instrument's frame feeds them directly: envelope per body summed over active strikes, strikes as `{ body, age, strikeU: 0.5 }` (the model has no position, so the mark sits at the body's middle and the page says so), no holds and no positions. Three differences keep the identity distinct and honest:
1. **No hands, no partial bands**: `vectors` empty, hold controls absent, the gesture ring never shown; the caption says this instrument is struck only.
2. **Bridges carry the coupling**: tube radius from the coupling (the anatomy builder already maps a radius; make the mapping linear over the Grove bounds and state it in the caption), so a chain and a triangle for the same target look different before any sound, and two triangles with different couplings look different in their bridges.
3. **A different stance, not a different drawing family**: labels 01, 02, 03 above the bodies as in the close view, a tighter single-instrument camera from the expressive close-view fit (`frameGroveView` on the scene's bounds), and the caption "the same drawing as the Groves, driven by a three-mass model; sizes, forms and sag are drawn, not derived". That is the honest statement: the forms are artistic, the motion is the model's.

## Touch, and what makes a changed connection discoverable
- **Strike by touching**: the close-view picker and tap adapter from Three Groves (`body-picking.js`, `body-tap.js`) already turn a deliberate tap on a shell or core into a strike with the same gate the buttons use; the numbered buttons stay as the keyboard and assistive route, and the page keeps the armed rule.
- **Touch a bridge to ask what it joins**: a tap on a bridge tube highlights it (`setBridgeHighlight`) and shows its coupling and the two bodies in the caption; it changes nothing. That answers "what is this connection" without a panel.
- **The same touch on the other anatomy**: the page remembers the last struck body; when a different candidate for the same target is prepared, one control "Touch 02 again here" strikes the same body, and the halos show where the ring goes this time. Discoverability comes from repetition across anatomies, not from sliders.
- **Follow the ring on by default here**: the workshop exists to show where the ring goes; the re-gained halo (0.15 + min(0.65, level × 10)) is the display that shows it, with the gain stated in the caption as it is on the Groves.

## What stays declared and what stays off
Picture captions: "Three lumped masses. Bodies and bridges drawn; sizes and sag are artistic. Halos and widening follow each body's low-band envelope with a stated display gain. Marks show touches at the body's middle; the model has no strike position." Off: any suggestion of material, air, radiation or room; audio-rate deformation; a moving camera; parameter editing on the picture.

## Evidence before it ships
A frozen fixture of the two default candidates (chain and triangle for the seed-3 target) at rest and at the display maxima; the transport snapshot driving the scene with the picture read at the same context time as the sound (the inverse transport already gives both); a native check that a tap on a shell strikes, a tap on a bridge highlights and does not strike, and that the ninth tap is reported as ignored; and the first-minute observation with people, asking what they touched next.
