# Bounded entry-path assessment: how a newcomer reaches the work (Fable, 2026-11-02; assessment only, no inverse controls)

Scope: the paths from the project home (`project.html`), the welcome page (`welcome.html`) and the catalogue (`src/project-catalog.js`, 30 entries) to the encounters, read from the files as they stand. Five concrete problems, each with a bounded fix.

1. **The site has three front doors, and the root is the least oriented.** `index.html` is the Resonator garden (an instrument), `welcome.html` is the three-door welcome, `project.html` is the collection and story. Anyone who types the site root meets an instrument with no orientation; the catalogue's own "Start here" points to `welcome.html`. Fix: make the root the welcome (move the garden to `resonator.html`, or have `index.html` carry the welcome and link the garden), and update the garden's links in the catalogue and welcome. One rename, a handful of links.
2. **The current edge is unreachable from the welcome.** `welcome.html` links Listen, Play, Rest, the Conservatory, Bring a score, the garden, the Expressive voices, "A gesture, another answer" and the project home, but not the inverse workshop, which `project.html` calls the current edge. Fix: one line in the welcome's "Inside the workshop" list, "A sound, several bodies", with its one-sentence scope.
3. **Eight catalogue links are dead on the published site.** The eleven listening studies include eight rendered studies under `renders/`, which are untracked (Michael's pending decision), so on GitHub Pages eight of thirty catalogue entries open nothing. Fix: either commit the renders (Michael's call) or mark those entries "local only" when the render is absent (a flag in the entry, or a fetch check at load), so the public catalogue never offers a dead link.
4. **One page, several names.** `ensemble.html` is "Make a small passage" on the current edge and "Three Groves — experimental" in the catalogue; "The Grove", "Three Groves" and "Grove voices" sit side by side with no stated relation; `index.html` is "Resonator garden" in the catalogue and "garden" in the welcome. Fix: one title per page used everywhere (welcome, current edge, catalogue, the page's own heading), with the relation stated once in the catalogue group description ("Grove voices is the study behind The Grove; Three Groves plays three of them together").
5. **Two different "starts".** `project.html`'s hero offers "Follow one voice" (expressive) and "Make a small passage" (ensemble) as the two ways in; the catalogue's "Start here" leads to the welcome, whose three doors are Listen, Play and Rest. A newcomer who arrives at the project home is sent to the most advanced pages first. Fix: the project home's first action should be the welcome's "Start here" (the three doors), with the current edge second, so the simplest encounters come before the deepest; no page changes beyond the hero's first link and label.

Not proposed: new controls anywhere, changes to the encounters themselves, or any claim in copy beyond what the pages do. Evidence for the fixes: click-through from the site root to each of the three doors and to the current edge in two steps or fewer; zero dead catalogue links on the published site; one name per page across the three surfaces.
