# Rest: caption text for the page (Fable draft, 2026-11-01; Astra integrates and edits freely)

**Choice (not default):** "Rest · exploration". Original remains the default; the Rest route is entered explicitly.

**Hint while playing:** "Hold a shell to strike it. Rest a hand on a shell (second pointer, or hold with a key) to damp it where you touch; lift to let it ring again."

**Model paragraph (what is physical, and what is imagined):**
"With Rest, the sound is still the same linear model of three bodies with beam partials coupled through their attachments. A resting hand adds one more element: a damper at the touched point, so the shell's motion there is resisted and its ring dies under the hand. The bridges carry that change to the other shells, which keep ringing differently, not necessarily more quietly. Lifting the hand restores the instrument exactly. Two hand strengths are offered, light and firm; they are chosen coefficients, not measurements of any real hand. The model has no hand mass or stiffness, no pressure sensing, no acoustic radiation and no room. Between gestures the sound evolves exactly, up to the arithmetic of the computer."

**Picture:** "Shell brightness follows the low modes of the audio state, sampled about every 21 ms; the picture does not invent motion between samples. The pale ring is your pending gesture; the small warm ring is a strike you made; a hand mark shows where a rest is in force. None of these show energy."

**Keeping:** "Everything you hear is decided on the audio clock; the saved passage uses those decisions, never the requested times. Keep the sound renders the passage again from the saved gestures and is identical to what was heard. Rejected gestures (too loud for the headroom, or past a limit) are not kept, and the page tells you when one is rejected."

**Limits, in one line:** "A passage holds up to 128 strikes and 256 rests and lasts at most two minutes; it then ends by itself with the same short fade as Quiet."
