Reviewer: Fable. Date: 2026-11-01.

## Verdict: approve
- **Flow**: Enter and Quiet precede the panel; the whole panel fits the first screen at 320 × 656; one visible gesture per version ("Strike one body. Stay with the ring. Try that same gesture in Original."), the prompt updating on the Original selection; reload returns Changed with Enter and no autoplay; the ordinary Rest route is untouched.
- **Copy**: "One declared rule stiffened connections according to modeled spring activity in your saved passage" names the rule; "both versions keep the same bodies and saved voice", "nothing changes while you play", "each choice starts a fresh performance", "new hands use Rest's Light and Firm strengths at the saved voice's strike positions", "your previous recording is replaced when you switch" and "no clear difference is a useful observation too" are all true and bounded. "Choose the version you want to spend time with" is a choice offered, not an appeal claim.

## Critique: the first thirty seconds
The arrival is honest and short; what it lacks is a target. "Strike one body" leaves the player to find where the change lives, and on a three-bridge grove two of the three connections changed little. The concrete encounter the current model affords is the connection that changed most: strike one of its ends, rest a firm hand on the other end while it rings, lift, then the same commands on the other version. That is what `composeRestPairInvitation(recipe, { against })` composes: a 6.7 s gesture at tempo 72 (ring 2.5 beats, hand across 3 beats, release 2.5 beats, Quiet), the bridge chosen by the largest |log ratio| between the two groves, identical commands on both recipes (tested), a prompt per section that names the ratio ("across the connection that changed most, 1 to 3 (×1.28)") and a settled line that says hearing no difference is a valid answer. With that, the thirty seconds are: arrive, read one line naming the connection, Enter, perform the gesture (7 s), switch, perform it again (7 s), and the player has a comparison they caused, with the structural fact beside it and no claim about what it is like. Through the existing "Show me a passage" in pair mode, as Astra proposes, no new control is needed.
