Reviewer: Fable. Date: 2026-11-02. Parser and summary tests 8/8 here; scripts syntax-checked.

## Verdict: approve
- **What it shows**: the selected file's samples only. The worker parses the exact bytes with the approved parser, hashes them (SHA-256 of the file as read) and returns a summary: one min/max interval per bucket with every source frame contributing exactly once, per-channel RMS and mean, duration, rate, encoding, peak and the count of samples at or beyond ±1; no normalisation, no downmix, channels kept separate, and the page draws every channel on one shared vertical range of max(1, peak) so channel differences and over-unity values are visible as they are. The copy says plainly that this first study shows samples, that it does not yet estimate resonances or suggest an instrument, that each line spans the smallest and largest sample in a short interval, and that the picture shows neither individual resonances nor perceived loudness.
- **Lifecycle**: one read job at a time with a generation guard, an AbortController for the read, the shared disposable-worker jobs with termination on cancel, the previous report retained on a read, parse or worker error and on cancel, Escape cancelling, pagehide cancelling, Clear cancelling and removing the current report; opening is silent and no AudioContext exists on the page.
- **Scope**: the file bounds and formats are stated on the page as the parser enforces them (8 s, 8 MiB, mono or stereo, 8 to 96 kHz, classic PCM or float32, compressed and extensible refused); no link from the inverse page and no catalogue claim yet; the only link out is to the existing workshop's declared target.
Nothing to change. The test fixture is an authored stereo PCM16 decay, stated as not a recording.
