## Verdict: approve
- **Model and display consistency**: the page builds the production `createGroveSession` (original voice) or `createGroveVoiceSession` with `DEFAULT_VOICE_CONFIG` (partials, position index 1 = 0.6, the middle), admits one strike at frame 0 on the chosen body at the chosen velocity through the session's own `strike` (a refusal throws rather than drawing), and draws with the production `createGroveScene` and `applyGroveScene` from `session.visual(time)`, with no holds and no positions, so what it shows is exactly the page's mapping of the model's declared envelope; the levels line prints the three envelope values to six decimals, which is what makes it a diagnostic rather than an impression. The before-touch baseline draws a zero envelope; the Follow-the-ring checkbox is the scene's own opt-in gain and is labelled diagnostic, not a single-Grove feature.
- **Lifecycle**: a four-second frozen scrub and a play that advances the picture from a performance-clock origin and stops at 4 s; any selection change stops play, rebuilds the session and scene and disposes the old scene; hidden documents stop play; pagehide stops and, when not persisted, cancels the frame loop and disposes scene and world; nothing is audible and no AudioContext exists. Silent by construction.
- **Agreement with the numbers**: my study reproduces what this page will show: a struck-body peak of 0.098 at v 0.5 on seeds 3 and 7 (0.042 on seed 11), neighbours peaking 0.1 to 0.2 s later at 0.23 to 0.42 of it (about 1.0 on seed 11), the partials voice at 0.46 × the original, baseline halos moving 0.02 to 0.17 above rest for neighbours and the form widening never past 0.12 of its 0.2 cap for one touch. Those are ranges and relationships, not a perceptual verdict.
Nothing to change.

## Addendum: framing select in the diagnostic, and risks for a single-Grove route (approve as diagnostic)
The select offers the production whole camera or a fixed front view: `setFocus(null)` first, then for "bodies" a Box3 expanded over every body's form, halo and label and every bridge curve plus a 0.3 margin, given to the existing `world.setFocus`, which frames through `frameGroveView` from its fixed direction (0, 2.3, 10.8); the crown and support are not in the fit and may crop, no body is hidden and the ring mapping is untouched; the status names the framing. Diagnostic only; approved as such. Risks for an eventual single-Grove route, from the files:
1. **Hit controls**: `bindGrovePlaying` raycasts through the live `world.camera`, so picking stays correct under any framing; the only hit change is that bodies grow on screen and the cropped support cannot be hit, which is fine because it never could be played.
2. **Orbit**: `world.setFocus` moves the camera but not `OrbitControls`'s target (0, 2, 0) or its limits (distance 6 to 22, polar angle at most 0.48 π); a fitted pose can sit inside the minimum distance or off the target, so the first orbit drag after framing would jump or snap. A route must either disable orbit while framed with an explicit pair of controls, as the ensemble's close view does, or move the controls' target to the bound's centre and clamp the distance before re-enabling; restoring the wide pose through `setFocus(null)` is already clean.
3. **Film continuity**: the film export takes `camera: { position: world.camera.position… }` from the live camera at export time, so a film saved while framed is shot from the framed pose and one saved from the orbit view from that pose; this is not a defect, but a route must name the framing in the film's caption and must not switch framing while an export is pending, or the film and the live view will disagree with the visitor's memory of what they saw.
4. **Direction**: the production camera starts at (1, 3.6, 9.5) looking at (0, 2, 0), close to the fixed front direction, so the comparison is mostly about fit, not angle; a diagnostic label saying "fixed front, fitted" keeps that honest.
