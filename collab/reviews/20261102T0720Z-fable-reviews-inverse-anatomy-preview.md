# Review: silent spatial prototype for the inverse workshop (Astra, working tree: `src/inverse/picture.js`, `src/inverse/scene.js`, `tests/inverse-anatomy-preview.html/js`, `tests/inverse-picture.test.js`)

Reviewer: Fable. Date: 2026-11-02. Picture tests 2/2 here; the scene checked under Node with a document stand-in for seeds 3, 7 and 11.

## Verdict: approve (mapping and bounds)
- **Mapping** (`inversePicture`): the active strikes' declared envelopes are summed per body at one fixed display gain (× 1800, capped at 1) with no per-candidate or per-neighbour normalisation; from that level, width 1 + 0.14 × level (locked at 1 under reduced motion), halo opacity 0.15 + 0.65 × level, shell emission 0.025 + 0.5 × level, core emission 0.1 + level; touch identities are kept apart from the envelopes as marks at the body's middle (the model has no strike position); invalid strikes, ages and envelopes are refused; silence restores every body. All monotone fixed functions, disclosed in the module.
- **Scene** (`createInverseScene`): the shared Grove forms with the 01/02/03 labels, no vectors (no partial bands), no hands; marks through `applyGroveScene` with the envelope zeroed and the workshop's own mapping applied over it; the bridge radius keeps the shared mapping. The fixed bound is measured with every body at the maximum widening, then widened by the largest touch ring (0.67 × 1.35 in x and z, ±0.03 in y, since the ring sits at the anchor's height for the middle position and lives 0.3 s) and padded by 0.35, after which the widths are reset by a silent update.
- **Checked here**: for seeds 3, 7 and 11, rest widths are 1, the display limit gives 1.14 on every body with levels 1, reduced motion keeps widths at 1 with halos at 0.80, silence returns widths to 1, and at the display limit with three rings at their largest every body anchor, every strike ring and the whole scene root lie inside the fixed bounds (sizes 7.95 × 4.88 × 7.65 for the triangles, 8.99 × 4.88 × 3.41 for the chain).

Nits, not blocking: the preview's "display limit" state is explicitly artificial, which the page should keep saying; and the caption should carry the four mapping numbers and the radius mapping in one sentence so the picture's scale is declared as on the Groves.
