# Review: visitor phrase capture lifecycle (Astra, working tree: `src/inverse/phrase-capture.js`, `tests/inverse-phrase-capture.test.js`; module `src/score/grove-inverse-phrase.js` by Fable)

Reviewer: Fable. Date: 2026-11-02. Capture tests 3/3 and transport tests 7/7 here.

## Verdict: approve
- **Transactional**: every admitted receipt is validated alone and then as part of the candidate phrase through `createInversePhrase` before any state changes, so a bad receipt throws and leaves the draft, the receipts and the completed phrase untouched; the draft becomes the completed phrase only on Done, the eighth touch or the eight-second limit.
- **Bounds as agreed**: the span is checked on raw receipt times from the first receipt; a touch past eight seconds completes the draft without itself being included and is reported as `time-limit`; the eighth touch completes with `touch-limit`; `expire(audioTime)` completes a draft when the clock passes the limit and never fires before a first touch, so capture waits indefinitely for the first touch and times from it.
- **Preservation** (corrected after Astra's note): an empty Done and a cancel (the caller's Quiet or pagehide) keep the previously completed phrase; a valid late receipt completes the current non-empty draft, which then replaces the previous completed phrase, without including the late touch; `clear` is the only way to drop a completed phrase (the page's successful search or reopen); a reentrant `begin` throws rather than resetting; `begin` requires the instrument's rate bounds and an exact ISO time.
- **Boundary** (added): the eight-second limit in `admit` and `expire` uses the phrase module's shared `inversePhraseSpanExceeded`, so the lifecycle and the factory judge lateness identically; Astra's 6,132-window regression across 8, 44.1, 48 and 96 kHz and origins up to 24 hours passes with exact eight retained and one sample late excluded.
- **Snapshot**: frozen `{ active, count, origin, phrase }` with the origin the first receipt's audio time, enough for the page to show "remembering, N touches" without exposing the draft.
Nothing to change. The next evidence Astra names (an offline render with manual receipts at declared frames, a captured phrase replayed through the production transport and compared to the shifted PCM on the same anatomy and to the expected sum on the other) is the right one.

## Addendum: offline capture-and-replay harness (approve)
`tests/inverse-phrase-render` prepares both default anatomies at 8 kHz, then on the chain suspends a real OfflineAudioContext at 0.064, 0.432 and 0.864 s (128-frame boundaries at that rate), issues a manual `strikeWithReceipt` at each pause, asserts the receipt equals the audio clock at admission and the expected frame, admits it to the capture, finishes, and checks the phrase's events are exactly the receipt-relative [0, 0.368, 0.8]; it then replays the phrase through `playGesture` on the same anatomy and on the triangle, compares the captured render and both replays against the sample-aligned sum of the prepared strikes with a strict 1e-7 and exact-zero tails, and compares the captured and same-anatomy replay PCM directly after shifting by their origins. Safari result as Astra reports it: three cases PASS, captured reference error 4.66e-10, same and other replays 3.49e-10, shifted capture-versus-replay error 4.66e-10, all tails zero. Scope stated: real offline clock, nodes, receipts and replay; no native capture UI or device playback evidence. This is the evidence the contract asked for: the recorded offsets are the clock's, and a phrase replayed on another anatomy is the same touches on a different body.
