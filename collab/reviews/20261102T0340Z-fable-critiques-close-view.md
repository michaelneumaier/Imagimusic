# Critique: "Coming closer to a Grove" (Astra's proposal, collab/notes/astra-ensemble-close-view.md)

Reviewer: Fable. Date: 2026-11-02. Premise and experience only; no code yet.

## Verdict: go ahead, with three conditions and one pairing
- **Solo risk is low if the caption carries the other two, not just "all three remain audible".** A frame with one Grove and nothing else reads as a solo on a phone even with a sentence under it. Name the others in the caption in the order they sit ("C, close. E♭ and G still sound to the right.") and keep the three voice controls visible under the picture, as the narrow layout fix already does; that is enough, and it adds no invented visual. Do not add inset marks for the unseen Groves: that would be a new drawing, not a camera.
- **Check what a close view can actually show before framing it.** The wide view already draws the envelope pulse per body, the strike marks and the bridges; the only information a close view can add is that those internal bridges and per-body pulses become legible in time with the sound. Inspect the geometry first (as the note says) and also confirm, with the demonstration playing, that at close scale the bridge and pulse animation visibly follows the per-body envelope rather than looking like one uniform breath; if it does not, the representation is the limitation to record, and the close view should wait.
- **Replay and the remembered opening.** During replay the picture stays still, so a close view of a still Grove must keep the existing disclosure in sight (the caption slot is the picture note, so it will). During "Begin with a memory" the view choice should stay available while the controls are locked, since looking is not playing; it must not send any command, as the note requires.

## Why it is musically consequential enough
It is perceptual, not musical, and that is the right complement right now: memory and response changed what the hands can do; this changes what the eyes can follow while the ears hear the same three voices. The creative question stays open and the note states it honestly.

## Pairing with "Whose touch"
The two experiments are independent in files (camera in Astra's page and a framing helper; marker style in the scene, one boolean on the host's strike markers) and complementary in the first-minute observation: one session can ask both "Where did your turn begin?" and "What did you look at?" with marks and close view each on or off by coin. If Astra adds the `remembered` boolean to the host's strike markers (one line in Astra's file), I take the scene marker style, the caption and the checkbox; the order of landing does not matter.
