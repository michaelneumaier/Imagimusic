How to read these bands

Colored bands: modes 1–4 in green, violet, amber and blue. White outline: their summed displacement bound. Both axes keep the same scale between bodies; transverse displacement is enlarged about 5,455× relative to length. The bands ignore phase cancellation and are not the tine’s instantaneous shape.

What is producing this sound?

An ideal point impulse changes the velocity of each retained bending mode according to its shape and modal mass. The signed pickup measures transverse velocity. Its output is multiplied by a fixed digital gain of 100 per meter/second; this is a listening conversion, not air pressure or a microphone simulation.

Every audition uses an impulse of 1 micro-newton second and lasts four seconds, with a final 25 ms fade. Longer natural decays are cut by that ending. There is no finite hammer contact, air loading, support loss or acoustic radiation. Damping is an assumed modal approximation. A strike at the ideal clamp is silent. Moving the pickup does not change the vibrating state.
