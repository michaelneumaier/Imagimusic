What these bodies actually model

Three lumped masses connected by ground springs and bridge springs, with a common damping rate. Each strike supplies 0.01 N·s. The left channel reads displacement at body 1; the right reads body 3. These are virtual pickups, not acoustic stereo space. Every candidate uses the same output scale. The listening-level control applies one shared playback gain afterward. At its maximum 4× setting, the conservative bound for eight overlapping strikes is below 0.51 of digital full scale; this is not a prediction of speaker or headphone loudness.

The spatial shells and suspension are artistic forms for the three-mass model, not simulated acoustic shells. Shell volume encodes mass and aspect encodes tuning. Spatial bridge radius is 0.012 + 0.04 × √(coupling / 0.04). Close views may crop other bodies and the ornamental support. The numbered diagram shows connections, not fabricated geometry. Bridge width indicates relative coupling. Its glow adds low-band displacement envelopes from active strikes; the summed envelope is multiplied by 1800 and capped at 1 for display. It is neither energy nor audio-rate deformation. Reduced-motion preferences keep body size fixed.

There are no beam partials, acoustic radiation, inferred materials, or claims about consciousness. The optimizer is bounded and does not promise a unique answer or a global optimum. The starting target is derived from seed 3 with equal masses; edited targets are your own declared numbers.

A separate study of constructed network twins holds one complete transfer response fixed. Those networks were constructed analytically; they were not found by this search.
