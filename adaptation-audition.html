How did the structure change?

Between versions, a declared rule stiffens the modeled connections according to their activity during the phrase. Activity counts work at both ends of each spring, including energy stored and released by that spring. It does not measure your attention or preferences.

The same phrase is played on each successive structure to produce the next change. These versions were prepared in advance; listening here never changes them. Every strike, hand interval, ending, and output gain setting remains the same.

This is an artistic rule applied to a reduced vibration model. Changes stop at bounded stiffness values; repeated use eventually saturates. The imagined shell meshes do not determine the sound, and there is no modeled room. We have not established whether this adaptation feels appealing to play.
