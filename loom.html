A bridge that
changes both voices.

Start with matching bodies. Strike left. Lower the coupling to zero: the right body stays still. Add coupling and energy can pass back and forth. Detune the right body to change how strongly it answers.

Each headphone channel picks up one body's displacement. The same two normal modes drive sound and picture. Width and glow show the displacement envelope, not audio-rate vibration. Each strike lasts up to 20 seconds, with a quarter-second fade at its end. Fast exchange above 8 Hz uses a labeled mean-level display.

REDUCED PHYSICAL MODEL · Two masses on springs, joined by a coupling spring, with mass-proportional damping. Geometry, pickup gain and visible shapes are interpretations; this is not a radiation or full elastic-bridge simulation.

Changing a control settles the previous strike. The next strike uses the new system.
