What this instrument models

Fresh encounters use Following the answer: the camera anticipates each answering body and stays with its phrase; overlapping answers share the frame. Turning camera following off holds the wide view. Earlier kept appearances retain their original framing. As in Porcelain performers, during a prepared unfolding, ceramic supports and bronze-headed strikers anticipate known contacts. They follow the score, while ideal impulses still generate the sound. Live touches are acknowledged immediately; no hammer collision or actuator dynamics are simulated. Earlier kept appearances reopen without these performers.

Make a piece uses phrase grammar 1: a seeded three-touch motif, a breath, and a repeated, softened or slightly delayed return. The six generated touches feed the existing five-pass arrangement. Spacing direction 1 changes their shared pulse while retaining the motif and accents. More space increases gaps by up to 25%, bounded by the four-second touch window; Closer uses 70% of the original pulse. It resynthesizes the same body at new event times, without stretching its audio. This is a bounded procedural composition on three fixed bodies. It does not invent new anatomy or interpret emotions. Explore adopts the generated source; saved encounter recipes preserve its exact touches, but not the generation seed or grammar label.

The blades share a 10 × 1 mm cross-section and use exposed lengths of 65, 80 or 95 mm, drawn at a common scale. The root is held; the other end is free. The ceramic support, bronze clamp, screws, materials and lighting are artistic construction. Their mass, compliance and contact are not part of the current uniform-beam sound model.

This is a real local WebGL scene, built from procedural geometry. Each touch supplies an ideal 0.1 micro-newton-second impulse at 85% of the span to the existing four-mode steel-tine model. A velocity pickup at the same point produces sound. No hammer contact or acoustic radiation. The body drawing stays still. The optional veil sums the absolute mode shapes weighted by their combined quadrature amplitudes. It bounds displacement between touches; it is not an instantaneous shape, measured motion, energy or sound pressure. Magnification is fixed, with no automatic normalization. Playing stops when the page leaves view. A completed phrase can return here on the original, longer or shorter blade. Only length changes: width, thickness, steel material, relative touch/pickup positions, event timing, impulse strengths and ending remain fixed. Each alternate is rendered anew; there is no pitch-shifting or continuous morph. Begin always starts a new phrase on the original 80 mm body. Unfold your phrase arranges five complete passes: an opening solo, three staggered answers, and the unchanged original solo. Each pass restarts its own mechanical state and retains the recorded ending. Answer voices have fixed gain 0.55 and artistic stereo panning; no acoustic radiation, normalization or added reverb. These variants are separate instruments, not mechanically coupled. The original and alternate versions can also open quietly in the existing replay page.
