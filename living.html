What this first scene establishes

The exposed blade follows the opening model’s 80 × 10 × 1 mm dimensions, drawn at a common scale. The root is held; the other end is free. The ceramic support, bronze clamp, screws, materials and lighting are artistic construction. Their mass, compliance and contact are not part of the current uniform-beam sound model.

This is a real local WebGL scene, built from procedural geometry. No automatic camera movement or sound. Playing, vibration presentation and the ensemble reveal are the next integration work.
