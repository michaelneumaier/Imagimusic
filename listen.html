What is modeled, and what is imagined?

The sound is a displacement pickup from two coupled masses and springs. Width and glow amplify the vibration envelope; the picture does not show audio-rate motion. The bridge is a connection indicator, not a meter of energy flow. Shapes and lighting are interpretations. This is a reduced physical model, not a simulation of sound radiating through a room.
