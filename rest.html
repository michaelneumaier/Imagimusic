A hand changes which vibrations survive. Rest on the body you struck, then try its neighbor. Follow what becomes audible when something else grows quiet. There is no particular feeling to reach.

What is physical, and what is imagined?

Nine coupled vibration modes generate this sound. A held hand adds localized viscous damping to the model while preserving its ongoing state. A hand also changes how the connected bodies share the ring; a neighbor may ring differently, not necessarily more quietly. Lifting removes that damping; it does not restore energy already lost. Light and Firm are two designed damping strengths, not measured hand pressure. There is no hand mass, nonlinear contact, acoustic radiation or room simulation.

The shells and their materials are artistic forms. The connecting springs belong to the sound model. Three vertical zones map to modeled strike and contact positions; they are not a simulation of the visible shell geometry. Width and glow amplify a coherent low-frequency response, not audio-rate motion or energy flow. A pale ring marks an acknowledged held contact, and a warm ring marks an acknowledged strike. The picture samples audio-thread state every 1024 frames (about 21 ms at 48 kHz), without inventing motion between samples. Drag empty space to turn the view.

The sound level is fixed per grove; dense gestures may be declined to preserve headroom. A passage holds 128 strikes and 256 contact starts, and ends gently after two minutes. Quiet releases all hands and records a short fade. The audio thread records when commands actually arrive. Audio interruptions pause its clock; release and Quiet commands wait for Resume if necessary.

Keep the sound rebuilds the finished model passage in a local worker and saves a stereo WAV. Keep the gestures saves the complete grove, voice, applied strike times, held contact intervals and ending as JSON. Listen back replays the saved sound with the model picture sampled at 60 frames per second. Quiet stops playback without changing the capture. This study currently has no film export or file import. Changing grove or beginning another replaces the current capture.
