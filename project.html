THE NORTH STAR

Watching should help
explain hearing.

Michael’s original idea begins with the wonder of Animusic: music embodied by strange machines. Imagimusic explores a further possibility—an invented body whose simulated behavior actually produces the sound.

A world that makes room for you

The ambition is a musical world that can sustain a complete listening experience after one action. Offer a small idea or join it through playing when you wish; ongoing input should never be the price of staying.

A place for attention

The spiritual and psychedelic direction lives in the encounter: a familiar gesture heard from another perspective, independent voices briefly finding a shared rhythm, a release that makes room for the next sound. Listening alone is a complete way to participate. There is no prescribed feeling to achieve.

Two kinds of truth

Some instruments use physical models of vibrating bodies. Others interpret synthesizer controls as visible mechanisms. Artistic geometry and amplified motion are identified as such. These are bounded models, not full acoustic objects or claims about consciousness.
