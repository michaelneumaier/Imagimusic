02 / THE PHYSICAL TINE

Give the sound a body.

Shorten it. Hear it rise.
Geometry determines these resonances.

Uniform clamped beam model. Geometry sets frequencies and mode shapes; strike magnitudes and the contact pickup are stylized. Material loss is estimated; the Damping control adds loss. Motion shows amplified envelopes, not audio-rate vibration. Dimension edits end the previous strike with a short fade.

03 / BELLOWS CHOIR

A breath becomes a voice.

Follow the air. Watch the iris open.
Every chamber carries its own envelope.

Ready for a breath

Hold Z X C V B N M, or hold a pad. Release to let the breath settle. Controls shape the next breath. This is an interpreted subtractive synth: the bellows follows the envelope, the iris follows filter cutoff on a log scale, and each pendulum follows its voice’s modulation clock. The chamber is not a simulated acoustic filter.

A working sketch of sonic anatomy. Sound and motion share resonant modes and decay. The garden interprets resonances; the tine derives them from geometry. Neither yet models full acoustic radiation.
