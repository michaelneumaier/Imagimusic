What does the experiment do?

A declared rule measures activity at the ends of each modeled spring during your saved passage, including energy stored and released by the spring. More active connections stiffen more, within fixed bounds. One change is prepared here, and only because you chose a file.

Every strike, resting hand interval, ending, and output gain setting stays the same. If the changed structure cannot carry every gesture, the comparison is refused. The rule does not measure your attention or preferences, and no change happens while listening.

These are reduced vibration models with virtual pickups, not acoustic simulations of the shell meshes or a room. We have not established whether this adaptation feels appealing to play.
