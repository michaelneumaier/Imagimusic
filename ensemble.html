Listening volume

The default boost is +20 dB (ten times the signal amplitude). Lower it here to suit your listening. Live playing, Listen back and derived returns share this level, remembered in this browser. Saved gestures and sound keep the original model level. A sample ceiling limits peaks; strong boosting can change loud transients.

Inside this encounter

Three intact instruments at C3, E♭3 and G3 receive the same strike at the middle position used in the listening study. Their response levels differ. Each has a fixed one-third share of the mix.

This local prototype has not yet been evaluated for output glitches or performance across devices. Quiet ends all voices together. Passages end automatically after two minutes; the browser reader accepts that duration plus the ending fade, at 8–48 kHz and up to 256 KiB.

The forms are artistic. Sound comes from reduced vibration models with virtual pickups. Widening and glow amplify low-frequency response, not audio-rate movement or acoustic radiation. Reduced motion keeps the forms still. The instruments do not interpret your intentions.
