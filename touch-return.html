The saved instrument and its limits

The original version reconstructs the saved sample-frame timing, gesture strengths, connection settings, output gain and ending. A comparison changes only its listed settings and uses the pickup recipe of that connection. The sound uses virtual displacement pickups on two coupled masses and springs. Shapes, widening, light and striker movement are interpretations; this is not acoustic radiation or simulated contact geometry.

The WAV copy is 16-bit PCM at the saved sample rate, without gain normalization. Browser and audio-device playback may resample. No file is uploaded.
