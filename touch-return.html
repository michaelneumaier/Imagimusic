File limits

The saved instrument and its limits

The original sample-frame timing, gesture force, connection settings, output gain and saved ending are reconstructed. The sound uses virtual displacement pickups on two coupled masses and springs. Shapes, widening, light and striker movement are interpretations; this is not acoustic radiation or simulated contact geometry.

The WAV copy is 16-bit PCM at the saved sample rate, without gain normalization. Browser and audio-device playback may resample. No file is uploaded.
