THE SELECTED RECORDING

Each vertical line spans the smallest and largest sample in a short interval. The picture preserves channel differences; it does not show individual resonances or perceived loudness.

File details and study limits

The file is read directly, without resampling, mixing channels or changing gain. A sample reaching ±1 does not by itself establish clipping. No sound is played and no file is uploaded.

Choosing three resonances and one decay from an arbitrary recording needs separate evidence. That analysis is being tested before it is connected to the inverse search.
