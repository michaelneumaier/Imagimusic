About this encounter

A fixed source supplies energy to the first body for two seconds. A resting hand changes damping at one of two positions; the other bodies respond through their connections. Quiet stops the source and fades the remaining sound.

This is a reduced physical model with idealized contacts and virtual pickups. The shell forms are artistic; their meshes do not determine the acoustics. Width and glow amplify a coherent low-frequency response, not audio-rate motion or energy flow. Saved reconstruction may differ by tiny numerical amounts across browsers.
