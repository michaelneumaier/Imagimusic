Inside this encounter

The opening returns on a timing drawn from this instrument’s low modes. Its answer placement uses a measured reference delay. Whether that feels like a pulse is open to your listening; there is no timing target to meet.

Sound comes from a reduced mass-and-spring model with virtual pickups. Shapes are artistic; visible widening and glow amplify a low-band response, not audio-rate movement or sound radiating through a room. The phrase is authored. The instrument does not interpret your intentions.
