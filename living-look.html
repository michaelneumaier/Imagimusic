What this comparison changes

The light buttons change lighting, exposure, background and surface colors. The composition buttons compare the original round with a spaced answer using the same three touches, bodies, voice gains and pans. Spaced entrances follow the full gesture span plus350ms (minimum800ms), allowing decays to overlap. The camera and response veils follow the selected score and audio clock. Changing composition pauses and returns to the one-second inspection point, retaining your camera choice. A held camera keeps all three bodies visible, including the silent ones.
