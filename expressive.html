What connects these sounds and movements?
Each note has independent pitch, pressure, brightness and timbre. Pressure shapes amplitude; brightness moves the lowpass cutoff and its visible iris; timbre changes filter resonance; pitch bends tilt the small lever. The bellows follows the note envelope. These shapes interpret a subtractive synthesizer, not an acoustic chamber or human anatomy.
A gold base ring marks a voice whose per-note expression has been detached: later per-note controls no longer reach it. Channel pitch bend still does. The numeric inspection retains the original 16- and 32-bit values; tiny changes can survive there without becoming perceptible.
The included studies are authored symbolic MIDI 2.0 expression scores; the optional local importer reads a bounded subset of MIDI Clip files. No MIDI device or live MIDI 2.0 transport is connected. Sound controls are sampled at 1 kHz with linear interpolation and exact message boundaries; visible values come from the analytic model. Live playback uses the prepared audio buffer. The saved WAV contains the entire passage.
Filter Q is expressed in dB for this Web Audio lowpass. This study uses the patch’s source and ADSR; its legacy filter-envelope, key-tracking and LFO controls are replaced by the expression score.
