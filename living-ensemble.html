About this listening study

The default 48-second composition distributes a 25-note rhythmic score among three continuously ringing steel-tine models. The optional remembered mode replaces two passages with answers derived from up to three accepted opening touches; their timing is compressed uniformly if they span more than three seconds. Without those touches, it plays the default score. Their lengths are 80, 95 and 65 mm. They share a timeline and a musical idea; they do not exchange mechanical energy. Different gestures come from a small seeded family, not an unlimited composition system.

The sound uses four vibration modes per body, ideal impulses and modeled velocity pickups. Acoustic radiation and collisions are not simulated. A fixed average combines the voices; listening gain and a sample ceiling sit downstream. The final fade changes output volume, not physical damping. The visible supports and spatial arrangement are artistic staging.
