    What to listen for

    Can you recognize the opening after its rhythm becomes closer and its accents soften? Do the connected exchanges feel purposeful? Do the separate chapters give you useful breathing room, or repeatedly interrupt the larger journey? Let the ending arrive before deciding.

    The connected version retains the five exchanges but removes their repeated solo openings and endings. It is substantially shorter. A developing motif introduces twelve exchanges: within each three-touch cell, the two rhythmic intervals can trade places and the accents move cyclically. The cell's beginning and end stay recognizable. The familiar rhythm returns before the familiar accents, then home restores the exact opening.

    Duration, exposure to the motif, rhythm and accents differ between these versions. This is a composition comparison, not a controlled preference test or an established improvement.

    How this sound is made

    Independent four-mode steel-tine passes produce the sound through a modeled velocity pickup. Ideal impulses excite them; some replies use ideal distributed damping. This is not acoustic radiation, contact-pad physics, or continuous shared-body vibration. Overlapping tails are mixed in full. All files use the same preview gain ×8 without normalization. The optional listening stage has a sample ceiling.

    The study is finite and authored around a seed. It does not yet adapt or invent new instruments. Opening the page is silent; leaving pauses playback. No preference verdict is implied.
