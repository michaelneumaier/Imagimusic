    What to listen for

    Can you recognize the opening after its rhythm becomes closer and its accents soften? Do the connected exchanges feel purposeful? Do the separate chapters give you useful breathing room, or repeatedly interrupt the larger journey? Let the ending arrive before deciding.

    The connected version retains the five exchanges but removes their repeated solo openings and endings. It is substantially shorter. Duration and repeated exposure to the motif both change, so this is a structural comparison, not a controlled preference test.

    How this sound is made

    Independent four-mode steel-tine passes produce the sound through a modeled velocity pickup. Ideal impulses excite them; some replies use ideal distributed damping. This is not acoustic radiation, contact-pad physics, or continuous shared-body vibration. Overlapping tails are mixed in full. Both files use the same preview gain ×8 without normalization. The optional listening stage has a sample ceiling.

    The study is finite and authored around a seed. It does not yet adapt or invent new instruments. Opening the page is silent; leaving pauses playback. No preference verdict is implied.
