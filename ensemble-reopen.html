A SAVED ENSEMBLE

    Kept sound uses the original model level and can be quieter than playback.

    The three recorded instruments share one timeline and a fixed mix. This reader rebuilds their sound; it does not animate or resume the performance.

    What comes back?

    Each instrument, accepted strike, resting contact, and shared ending is reconstructed from the ensemble file. Silent parts stay silent. Derived returns are labelled separately and show their declared source fingerprint; the source file is not supplied here for verification. This reader accepts ensemble version 1 files and derived-return version 1 wrappers up to 256 KiB, at 8–48 kHz, with an ending up to two minutes after the first gesture, followed by a fade of up to one second.

    Long passages can take time to rebuild. Elapsed preparation time is shown while opening, and Cancel opening stops the work. A previous successfully opened ensemble stays available if you cancel or the new file fails. Higher sample rates remain outside this reader’s limits.

    Sound comes from reduced vibrating-body models and virtual pickups, with no room or acoustic-radiation simulation. The three Groves are independent instruments; their sounds are mixed, not physically coupled to each other.

    Listening volume

    The default boost is +20 dB. Lower it to suit your listening; this browser remembers your choice. Saved audio keeps the original model level. A sample ceiling limits peaks; strong boosting can change loud transients.
