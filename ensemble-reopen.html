A SAVED ENSEMBLE

    The three recorded instruments share one timeline and a fixed mix. This reader rebuilds their sound; it does not animate or resume the performance.

    What comes back?

    Each instrument, accepted strike, resting contact, and shared ending is reconstructed from the ensemble file. Silent parts stay silent. This reader accepts ensemble version 1 files up to 256 KiB, at 8–48 kHz, with a total duration of up to 30 seconds including the ending fade.

    The underlying archive format permits longer passages and higher sample rates; this browser reader uses narrower limits to bound reconstruction work. Opening can be cancelled.

    Sound comes from reduced vibrating-body models and virtual pickups, with no room or acoustic-radiation simulation. The three Groves are independent instruments; their sounds are mixed, not physically coupled to each other.
