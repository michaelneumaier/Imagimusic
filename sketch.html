What has been constructed?

A decay variation is your change to the construction, not a new fit to the recording. It retains the supplied oscillation frequencies and quadratures, and reconstructs damping, stiffness and initial velocity accordingly. It is not a damping-only edit to a fixed physical body. The pickup gain stays the same as its original interpretation.

Each mode is an independent damped oscillator with arbitrary unit mass. Activating it sets displacement and velocity together. There are no inferred bridges, materials, acoustic chambers or radiation model. Matching a recording does not establish a unique physical instrument.

The authored shared gesture uses offsets 0, 0.9 and 2.1 seconds with strengths 0.35, 0.2 and 0.35. It is scheduled together on the audio clock with a 50 ms lead; its last ring ends about 6.15 seconds after you choose it. Quiet cancels future activations as well as fading current ones. Each activation lasts four seconds, with a final 25 ms playback fade. Quiet applies its own 25 ms fade. Overlap is limited by a conservative output bound, so a rapid new activation may be refused until earlier ones finish. The browser or device may resample output.

The source study uses a fixed 0.1 listening gain; this sketch uses a displayed per-model pickup gain and a fixed 0.35 activation strength. The two pages are not level-matched. Changing interpretations stops the prepared voice only when you explicitly prepare the replacement.
