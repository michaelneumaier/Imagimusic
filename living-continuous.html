About this listening experiment

This is a 48-second, seeded rhythmic composition on one continuously ringing steel-tine model. Its eight passages are authored transformations of a small gesture. In “The original return,” an added touch changes the ringing sound while the score continues unchanged. In “Let my gesture return,” the first three accepted touches before six seconds supply two later answers, at 18.5 and 36.5 seconds. Their relative timing is preserved or uniformly shortened to fit three seconds; their strengths soften. Later touches still sound without changing those answers. With no touches, both styles play the same score. This is a defined musical transformation, not an interpretation of your feelings.

The sound uses four vibration modes, ideal impulses and a modeled velocity pickup. It does not model acoustic radiation or collisions. The timeline shows score position, not physical motion or emotion. The final fade changes output volume, not the instrument’s physical damping.

Opening is silent. Leaving or hiding the page stops playback. Quiet completes a short capture; cancellation or interruption does not. A completed capture is checked in a background worker before its JSON download appears. Kept continuous records can be reconstructed separately for replay and audio download.

Experimental browser integration: native playback and listening quality have not yet been verified. The previous kept performance remains available if a new attempt fails.
