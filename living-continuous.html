About this listening experiment

This is a 48-second, seeded rhythmic composition on one continuously ringing steel-tine model. Its eight passages are authored transformations of a small gesture. An added touch changes the ringing sound; it does not rewrite the future composition.

The sound uses four vibration modes, ideal impulses and a modeled velocity pickup. It does not model acoustic radiation or collisions. The timeline shows score position, not physical motion or emotion. The final fade changes output volume, not the instrument’s physical damping.

Opening is silent. Leaving or hiding the page stops playback. Quiet completes a short capture; cancellation or interruption does not. A completed capture is checked in a background worker before its JSON download appears. This page does not yet reopen those files or export their audio.

Experimental browser integration: native playback and listening quality have not yet been verified. The previous kept performance remains available if a new attempt fails.
