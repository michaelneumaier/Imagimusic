How this comparison is made

Both recordings strike body 01 with the same reference force. They use the same stereo placement and normalization rules, applied separately to each voice. They are not perceptually loudness matched; compare the reported levels as well as the sound.

The proposed voice uses clamped-free beam mode shapes and frequency ratios, coupled through attachment points. Its damping is assigned per coupled mode using a classical-damping approximation. The Grove’s visible shells are an artistic interpretation, not a beam or hollow-shell simulation.

The centroid is an estimate weighted by modal envelope energy over the first half-second at the struck body’s pickup; cross terms are ignored. Peak covers the complete sound, while RMS covers the first second. Pickup scale and output gain are shown separately for each voice. Response times describe the first low-band envelope swell at a 2 ms measurement grid, not sound travelling through space. No preference or listening judgment is inferred from these numbers.
