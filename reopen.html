What comes back?

The saved instrument, applied strike times, resting contacts, and Quiet ending are rebuilt together. Timing and sound level are preserved. The reader accepts finished Rest version 2 passages up to two minutes, with sample rates from 8 to 96 kHz, in JSON files up to 256 KiB.

These are imagined forms around a coupled vibration model with viscous damping contacts and virtual pickups. There is no hand mass, acoustic radiation, or room simulation. This page replays the saved gestures; it does not resume recording them.

Listening volume

The default boost is +18 dB. Lower it to suit your listening; this browser remembers your choice. Saved audio keeps the original model level. A sample ceiling limits peaks; strong boosting can change loud transients.
